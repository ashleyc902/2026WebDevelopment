The Kakapo

The Kakapo is the largest, flightless parrot native to New Zealand. The Kakapo holds the world record of being the heaviest parrot as some male Kakapos can weigh up to 8.8 pounds. By using their strong legs, Kakapos can climb up high into trees rather than flying. Kakapos are a critically endangered species as there are only 325 left in the world dispite their average lifespan being 60 to 90 years.
